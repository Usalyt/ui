## Layout behavior

Centered alert dialogs are capped to the viewport height with a 2rem inset and scroll their content, keeping the title and confirmation actions reachable on short or zoomed viewports. `AlertDialogFooter` applies a compact 8px gap at every breakpoint: actions stack in reverse DOM order on mobile and become a right-aligned row from `sm`. Caller `className` overrides still apply.
